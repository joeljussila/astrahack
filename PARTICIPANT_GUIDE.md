5. Hackathon Rules
Open Source: Repositories must be public. If you are adding a feature to your startup, make sure it's in an isolated repository that you can share publicly

Team Size: A maximum of four team members per team. Solo participants are allowed.

Demo Requirements: Your demo must only highlight the specific features, code, and functionality that your team built during the hackathon. Judges must be able to clearly identify what was created during the event. Failure to clearly identify your original contributions will result in immediate disqualification.

Banned Projects: Projects will be disqualified if they: violate legal, ethical, or platform policies, use code, data, or assets you do not have the rights to.

8. Judging Process
Teams will submit their projects through the CV platform by 5:30PM. Each submission includes a short 1 minute demo video. After submissions close, a group of judges from OpenAI will review the projects and select the 5 most interesting, useful, creative, and technically ambitious projects for the stage.

Selected teams will then demo on stage in front of all attendees at 6:45PM. Each team will have 3 minutes to live demo their project, plus a 2 minute judge Q&A.

Once all finalists present, judges will convene for a final deliberation. After determining the top 3 winners, there will be an award ceremony concluding the event.

Judging Criteria
GPT-6 Astra in Development (25%)
How did the team use the model to build their project? Did they leverage new features and capabilities efficiently? How did they work with the model during development?

GPT-6 Astra in Project (25%)
How did the team use GPT-6 Astra in their end project? Does the project incorporate new features to unlock advanced capability? Is the project a novel use of the model?

Live Demo (25%)
Is the concept of this project novel? Is it presented and demo'd well? Is this a cool project to watch? Is this ONLY a demo, and NOT just a presentation, figma file, or anything else?

Technicality (25%)
How well has the team implemented the idea? How technical is their usage and implementation? Is this a project with sound engineering?
